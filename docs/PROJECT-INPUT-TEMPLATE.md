## Editorial rules

- Ask for missing factual information instead of inventing it.
- Distinguish clearly between client work, personal work and speculative work.
- Do not fabricate KPIs, campaign reach, client approval, awards or commercial results.
- Write concise English and Vietnamese copy that sounds cinematic and professional.
- Avoid repetitive adjectives and generic AI language.
- Display headlines do not end with a period.
- Body paragraphs may use normal punctuation.
